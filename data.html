Reflection

This dashboard provides a look into global music streaming trends, highlighting the specific sonic attributes and release patterns that define massive hits. The total of 489.46 billion streams across the dataset shows the massive scale of music consumption, with top tracks like "Perfect" topping the charts. Musically, artists and producers seem to heavily favor the Major key for maximum appeal to all audiences, this is shown in the top ten songs all being in the major key. The seasonal release data shows noticeable fluctuations throughout the calendar year, indicating that artists strategically time their drops to maximize listener engagement. However, there are numerous flaws in the data such as the pie chart having 31 different sections and having to be scaled up to be legible. Additionally, the data shows major differences between streaming platforms, with songs being added to playlists on Spotify an average of 5,200 times compared to just under 68 times on Apple Music. This highlights that most of the data is from Spotify thus all records from Apple music being outliers. In conclusion, while the data shows insights into the key of the music in relation to the top streamed songs, however the heavy bias towards Spotify users makes the data disproportionate.
